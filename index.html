Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Calculator Data Stays in Your Browser

All numbers you enter into the Building Profit Checker (including contract prices, labor hours, hourly rates, overhead percentages, and building names) are calculated entirely inside your web browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your financial or building inputs to our servers.

2. Information We Collect

If you choose to join our early access list, we collect only the information you voluntarily submit in the sign-up form: your email address and, optionally, the number of buildings you manage.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address solely to notify you when the full Rinsemark platform is ready and to share relevant product updates. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third-party marketers.

4. Third-Party Services

Early access form submissions are processed securely through Formspree solely for the purpose of delivering your sign-up details to us.

5. Unsubscribing & Data Deletion

You can unsubscribe from updates or request that we delete your email address from our list at any time by replying directly to any email we send you.
